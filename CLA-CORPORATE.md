# Inkuna Corporate Contributor License Agreement

**Version 1.0, effective November 23, 2026**

This agreement lets a company or other organization (the "Corporation")
authorize the people it names to contribute to Inkuna (the "Project"), which
is maintained by Madcha LLC ("Madcha"). It is a license, not an assignment:
**the Corporation keeps the copyright to its Contributions**.

Each person who contributes on the Corporation's behalf also signs the
[Individual Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md), which covers anything
they contribute that the Corporation does not own.

## 1. Definitions

**"Corporation"** means the legal entity that signs this agreement, together
with every entity that controls it, is controlled by it, or is under common
control with it. "Control" means owning more than fifty percent of the
outstanding shares or securities that vote to elect an entity's management,
or the power to direct its management by contract or otherwise.

**"Designated Contributor"** means a person listed in Schedule A, as updated
under Section 8.

**"Contribution"** means any original work of authorship, including any
change or addition to an existing work, that a Designated Contributor
intentionally submits to Madcha for inclusion in the Project and that the
Corporation owns or may license. "Submit" means any form of communication
sent to Madcha or its representatives for the purpose of discussing or
improving the Project, including pull requests, patches, issues, comments, and
messages on the Project's mailing lists or chat. It does not include
communication conspicuously marked as "Not a Contribution."
